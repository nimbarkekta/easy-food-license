Links and QR codes

Use a different link for each ad. Every application then shows where the customer came from (Admin page, "From:").

How to use
  • Instagram / Facebook ad: in Ads Manager, choose the goal "Traffic" and paste the matching link as the Website URL. You can also put the QR image inside the ad picture.
  • Instagram bio or Facebook page button: use the same links.
  • Posters, banners, visiting cards: download the "Poster" QR and print it at least 3 cm wide.
  • WhatsApp status / groups: copy the WhatsApp link and share it.
